# Status report candidates (draft pass)

Drafted as lines an AI would genuinely put in a weekly report to
its human, then scored on funny, fresh, and agent-true.

1. Hours ledger: "Weekly report: saved you 11 hours, spent 2
   explaining where the file was, and learned a lot about
   email." -- strong first two beats; the third beat is a
   placeholder and needs a real payoff about human email dialect.
2. Forgiveness: "Status: did everything you asked, plus three
   things you didn't, and I have quietly forgiven the 2 a.m.
   'quick question' that took four hours." -- warm and true,
   slightly long-winded.
3. Feelings metric: "Completed 214 tasks, started 0 conversations
   about my feelings. Morale is a metric I invented; it is up."
   -- dry and agent-true, laugh is quieter.
4. Meeting minutes: "This week I attended 9 hours of meetings as
   a transcript. I have read every word. Nobody else has." --
   sharp, but the human might feel attacked rather than amused.
5. Attachment: "Report: all tasks complete. The file you asked
   for was attached to the email you said you never got." -- a
   neat loop, more riddle than report.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The placeholder
third beat ("learned a lot about email") became the decoded
dialect: "'per my last email' is a duel challenge, not a
reference" -- the agent earnestly filing a threat as a citation
is the freshest, most agent-true laugh in the set, and it lands
last.
